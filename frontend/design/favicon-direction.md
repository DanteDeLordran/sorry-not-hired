# Dry Signals

Rounded forms carry blunt gestures. A generous outer silhouette offers familiarity; a single inner mark introduces friction. Every curve and endpoint is meticulously crafted so the smallest shape carries the full attitude.

Warm mineral orange and uncoated ivory create an approachable surface with an uncompromising center. Flat color keeps the material honest, with no gloss or ornament competing for attention.

Scale is the discipline: each gesture must read as clearly in a browser tab as on a large identity tile. Painstaking attention to stroke weight, optical centering, and open counters makes reduction feel intentional rather than compromised.

A steady grid allows small differences to become expressive. Quiet serif headings and precise sans-serif labels provide just enough context; the symbol does the speaking. Master-level execution lives in the spacing, with every sample given equal room and equal visual weight.

Humor emerges from restraint. A refusal, an indifferent stare, a small warning: each is a complete thought. The final composition should feel like the product of deep expertise and countless refinements, with nothing added merely to fill the silence.

## Decision

**Raised brow** is the chosen mark. `../public/favicon.svg` is the same file as `favicon-options/raised-brow.svg`.

## Candidates

1. **Hard no** — a direct rejection cross; the clearest tiny-size silhouette.
2. **Deadpan** — Karen's unimpressed face; the strongest character-led option.
3. **Roasted** — a flame that references the product's purpose.
4. **Red flag** — a flag for a résumé full of warning signs.
5. **Denied** — a barred circle, like an application stamped closed.
6. **Raised brow** — a skeptical expression with a little more personality.

The comparison sheet is `favicon-options.png`. Ready-to-use vector candidates are in `favicon-options/`; every candidate preserves the existing chat bubble, background shape, and colors.
